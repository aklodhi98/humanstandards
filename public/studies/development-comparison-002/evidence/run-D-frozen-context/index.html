Hard-waste collection

Book one pickup from your home

Wattle Bay residents can use this service for one collection of bulky household items. There is no payment in this booking.

Before you begin

Have these ready:

  • your contact and street address details
  • the type of waste you are putting out
  • a suitable collection window

Place items on your property boundary only for the collection window you choose.
