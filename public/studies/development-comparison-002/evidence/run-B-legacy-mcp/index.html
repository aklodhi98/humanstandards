Step 1 of 4

Before you start

This service records one hard-waste pickup booking in this browser.

01

What you can book

Choose one category: furniture, whitegoods or garden waste. You will also choose one available collection window.

02

What you will need

Your name, Wattle Bay street address, email address and Australian mobile number.

i

Saved on this device only

This static service does not send email or SMS, take payment, or update a council system. Your confirmed booking is kept only in this browser until you clear it.
