title: Localization & Cultural Variations
description: Design for language, formats, and cultural metaphors; support RTL and expansion.
---
## Practices

- Externalize strings; account for 30–50% text expansion.
- Support RTL; mirror layout appropriately.
- Localize date/number/currency; avoid culture-specific idioms.
