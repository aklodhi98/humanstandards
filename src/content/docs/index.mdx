## Why Human Standards?

The digital age has unfurled a world of unprecedented connection, innovation, and access. Yet, as this digital landscape expands, a critical question emerges: **Is our technology truly serving humanity's best interests?**

Too often, digital experiences feel extractive rather than empowering, divisive rather than unifying, and overwhelming rather than supportive of our well-being.

**Current Focus:** This project currently focuses on **digital interfaces** (web, mobile, desktop). While the underlying principles apply universally, our implementation guidance and code examples are tailored to digital product design. See our [Scope & Roadmap](/human-overview/scope-and-roadmap/) for planned expansion into voice, VR/AR, robotics, and other domains.
