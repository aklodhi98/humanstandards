---
title: Onboarding Playbook
description: Patterns and tactics to help users reach first success quickly and confidently.
---
## Define success

- Identify the activation event (time-to-first-value).
- Map the shortest path from signup to success.
- Personalize paths by role/goal when possible.

## Reduce friction

- Progressive profiling: ask later, not sooner.
- Smart defaults and templates; import or sample data.
- Inline guidance: hotspots, checklists, examples.

## Teach by doing

- Interactive walkthroughs over static tours.
- Safe sandbox; undo available; no irreversible steps.
- Celebrate progress; show remaining steps and ETA.

## Safety & trust

- Transparent permissions and data usage.
- Clear pricing/limits; trial boundaries visible.
- Human help: chat, email, or office hours paths.

## Measurement

- Track activation rate, TTFV, drop-off points.
- Survey early cohorts (CES, PMF signals).
- Iterate: A/B test copy, order, and defaults.
