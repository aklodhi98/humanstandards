---
title: Error Types (Slips vs Mistakes)
description: Slips are execution errors; mistakes are decision errors — design for both.
---
## Patterns

- Prevent slips with constraints, confirmations, and forgiving inputs.
- Prevent mistakes with clearer models and staged decisions.
