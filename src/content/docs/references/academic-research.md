Human factors and HCI research builds the evidence base for everything in this guide. Knowing where to find primary sources helps you go deeper, make better arguments, and stay current.

## Key conferences

### CHI (ACM Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems)

The flagship HCI conference. Covers interaction design, accessibility, user research methods, and emerging technologies. Proceedings available through ACM Digital Library.

- Website: https://chi.acm.org/
- Access: https://dl.acm.org/conference/chi

### HFES (Human Factors and Ergonomics Society Annual Meeting)

Broader than HCI — covers aviation, healthcare, industrial design, and cognitive ergonomics. Strong on safety-critical systems.

- Website: https://www.hfes.org/events/annual-meeting

### ASSETS (ACM SIGACCESS Conference on Accessible Computing)

Focused specifically on accessibility research. Covers assistive technology, inclusive design, and disability studies.

- Website: https://assets.acm.org/

### CSCW (ACM Conference on Computer-Supported Cooperative Work)

Collaboration, social computing, and multi-user systems. Relevant for shared workspaces, communication tools, and remote collaboration.

- Access: https://dl.acm.org/conference/cscw

## Key journals

### Human Factors

The journal of the Human Factors and Ergonomics Society. Covers physical ergonomics, cognitive factors, and system design.

- Publisher: SAGE
- Website: https://journals.sagepub.com/home/hfs

### ACM Transactions on Computer-Human Interaction (TOCHI)

Peer-reviewed journal for HCI research. More in-depth than conference papers.

- Access: https://dl.acm.org/journal/tochi

### Ergonomics

International journal covering physical and cognitive ergonomics across domains.

- Publisher: Taylor & Francis
- Website: https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/terg20

### International Journal of Human-Computer Studies

Covers both theoretical and applied HCI research.

- Publisher: Elsevier
- Website: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/international-journal-of-human-computer-studies

## Finding papers

### Google Scholar

Best general-purpose search. Use site filters for specific venues:
- `site:dl.acm.org "accessibility"` for ACM papers
- `site:nngroup.com "usability testing"` for practitioner research

### ACM Digital Library

All ACM conference and journal papers. Free access to some content; institutional access for full archive.

- Website: https://dl.acm.org/

### Semantic Scholar

Good for finding related papers and seeing citation networks.

- Website: https://www.semanticscholar.org/

## Citing sources

When referencing research, include:

- **Author(s)** — Last name, initials
- **Year** — Publication year
- **Title** — Paper or book title
- **Venue** — Conference, journal, or publisher
- **DOI or URL** — Persistent link

Example:
> Card, S. K., Moran, T. P., & Newell, A. (1983). *The Psychology of Human-Computer Interaction*. Lawrence Erlbaum Associates.

For web resources, include access date if the content may change.
