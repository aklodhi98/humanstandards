# Study 001 reviewer call closed

Study 001 has concluded as an exploratory, coordinator-reviewed pilot. We did not ask the two planned independent reviewers to complete the frozen rubric after the coordinator walkthrough exposed material cross-condition failures and no consistently superior experience.

No independent review scores were collected and the preregistered human-centred quality outcome is reported as missing—not replaced with the coordinator's observations.

[Read the Study 001 case study and detailed findings →](/studies/study-001-case-study/)

Thank you to everyone who considered contributing. A future validation study will use a new task and fresh outputs so that independent reviewers assess a prospective holdout rather than evidence already used to improve the MCP.
