## Purpose

Humanstandards is a practical guide to designing with human constraints in mind — perception, cognition, ergonomics, accessibility, and behavior.

## Who this is for

- Designers, engineers, PMs, content strategists
- Anyone who needs concrete, evidence-informed guidance

## How to use

- Quick Reference: grab key numbers and thresholds.
- Topic Guides: understand constraints and apply patterns.
- Checklists & Playbooks: ship with confidence.

## Scope and terms

- Human Factors (HF/E): optimizing fit between human, task, and environment.
- Accessibility (a11y): ensuring people with disabilities can use products.
- Ethics: avoid dark patterns; build trust by default.

## Versioning and changes

- We track updates in CHANGELOG and annotate notable standard changes.
