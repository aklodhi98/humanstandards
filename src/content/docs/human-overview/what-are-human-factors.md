## Definition

Human Factors and Ergonomics (HF/E) is the discipline focused on designing systems that fit human capabilities and limitations — physical, cognitive, and organizational.

## Goals

- Reduce errors and harm
- Improve performance and efficiency
- Increase accessibility and inclusion
- Enhance satisfaction and trust

## Domains

- Physical (anthropometrics, biomechanics, environment)
- Cognitive (attention, memory, decision-making)
- Organizational (teams, procedures, culture)

## Outputs

- Principles and design guidance
- Prototypes and evaluations
- Standards alignment and safety cases
