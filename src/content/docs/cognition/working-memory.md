---
title: Working Memory
description: Practical limits mean UIs must chunk information and externalize memory.
---
## Constraints

- Practical capacity: ~3–4 chunks; easily disrupted.
- Sensitive to stress and multitasking.

## Design tactics

- Chunk content; progressive disclosure; clear hierarchy.
- Recognition over recall; visible context and history.
- Defaults and sensible autofill; save state and drafts.
