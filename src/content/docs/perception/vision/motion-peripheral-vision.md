## Guidelines

- Reduce unexpected motion; offer “reduce motion” preference support.
- Use subtle, short-duration easing; avoid parallax on content.
- Motion near edges grabs attention; reserve for important events.

## Vestibular considerations

- Large parallax/zoom can induce motion sickness.
- Provide instant transitions when prefers-reduced-motion is set.
